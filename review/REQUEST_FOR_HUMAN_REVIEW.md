# Call for independent mathematical review / Appel à relecture mathématique

We are seeking independent human reviewers for a candidate proof concerning
the asymptotic volume deficit of translated lattice hulls in a
three-dimensional Euclidean ball.

Nous sollicitons une relecture humaine indépendante d’une preuve candidate
concernant le déficit volumique asymptotique de l’enveloppe convexe des points
d’un réseau translaté dans une boule de dimension trois.

A complete review is not required. The following independent review tracks are
especially valuable:

- homogeneous dynamics: Moore-Ratner argument and affine equidistribution;
- geometry of numbers: flatness theorem and the uniform tail estimate;
- convex geometry: radial reduction, parabolic hull and localization;
- probability: weak convergence, uniform integrability and limiting
  expectation;
- numerical reproducibility: finite-radius experiments, treated separately
  from the proof.

Please use the GitHub **Mathematical review report** form and identify the exact
release, section and equation. Critical findings and counterexamples are
explicitly welcome.

The development and internal audits used ChatGPT and Codex in an integrated
way, with more occasional consultations of Claude AI, DeepSeek, Z.ai and Kimi.
None of these systems is an author, co-author or independent human reviewer.
