## Description

This working manuscript proposes an asymptotic theorem for the relative volume
deficit of the convex hull of a fixed translate of the integer lattice inside a
three-dimensional Euclidean ball. The candidate result states that
R^(3/2) delta_V(R, xi) converges to a positive finite universal constant,
independent of the fixed translation xi, represented as an expectation over
affine unimodular lattices. The proof combines radial reduction, a parabolic
local model, affine homogeneous dynamics, a compact localization argument and
uniform integrability obtained from a flatness-based tail bound.

The project used substantial, integrated assistance from ChatGPT and Codex for
mathematical exploration, drafting, verification, consolidation, programming
and internal critique. Claude AI, DeepSeek, Z.ai and Kimi were consulted more
occasionally for exploration or cross-checking. No AI system is presented as
an author or human reviewer. The internal audit is not independent and the work
has not yet received human peer review. ROYER Patrick — Projet Dynagénèse is
the human author and takes responsibility for the deposited claims and files.
This release explicitly requests independent human verification, especially of
the Moore-Ratner affine-equidistribution argument, the flatness tail estimate
and the variable-domain localization step. The release does not claim an
explicit value of the limiting constant, a quantitative convergence rate or a
surface-area analogue.
