# Independent mathematical review guide

A complete review is not required. A focused report on one lemma or one proof
transition is valuable if its scope is explicit.

## Review protocol

1. Record the immutable release tag and, once available, the Zenodo DOI.
2. Identify the document, section, displayed equation or exact claim.
3. Restate the claim as you understand it.
4. Classify your conclusion as one of:
   - verified;
   - correct after a minor clarification;
   - repairable gap;
   - potentially blocking gap;
   - counterexample or false statement;
   - unable to determine.
5. Give the argument, reference, missing hypothesis, calculation or
   counterexample supporting that conclusion.
6. Explain whether the finding is local or affects the main asymptotic claim.

Primary sources are preferred. Please distinguish a checked implication from a
plausibility judgement. A mathematical objection should be filed as an issue,
even if a pull request also proposes a textual repair, so that the review
history remains visible.

## Independence and acknowledgement

Reviewers may disclose relevant expertise and possible conflicts of interest.
With explicit permission, substantive reviewers will be acknowledged in a
later release. A reviewer may remain unnamed.
